Privacy Policy – Mic Mute Hotkey

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Mic Mute Hotkey is developed by Murtadha Qahtan (Microsoft Store publisher “rul”).

Mic Mute Hotkey does not collect, record, store, transmit or share any personal data or audio.

  • Offline only. The app never connects to the internet. It has no analytics, telemetry, advertising, crash reporting, accounts or third-party SDKs.
  • Microphone. The app mutes and unmutes your microphone through the Windows audio settings (the same switch as in Windows Sound settings). It does not record audio. The optional input level meter in the Settings window opens the microphone only while that window is open, to read the current loudness peak for the meter bar; the sound itself is never stored or sent. Windows may show its “microphone in use” icon while the meter is visible. You can turn the meter off.
  • Keyboard. For push-to-talk and push-to-mute, the app watches for the one hold key you chose while that mode is on. Other keys are ignored immediately; no keystrokes are logged, saved or sent.
  • Settings (shortcuts, indicator position, language, options) are stored in a local file on your PC and are removed when you uninstall the app. An optional debug log of app events (never key presses or audio) stays on your PC.
  • Children. The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Microsoft Store; the developer receives no payment details.

Política de privacidad (resumen)

Mic Mute Hotkey no recopila, graba, almacena, transmite ni comparte datos personales ni audio. Funciona sin conexión, sin telemetría ni anuncios. Solo activa o desactiva el silencio del micrófono de Windows; el medidor de nivel opcional lee el volumen solo mientras la ventana de configuración está abierta y no guarda el sonido.

Política de privacidade (resumo)

O Mic Mute Hotkey não coleta, grava, armazena, transmite nem compartilha dados pessoais ou áudio. Funciona offline, sem telemetria nem anúncios. Apenas liga ou desliga o mudo do microfone do Windows; o medidor de nível opcional lê o volume só enquanto a janela de configurações está aberta e não guarda o som.

Datenschutz (Kurzfassung)

Mic Mute Hotkey erfasst, speichert, überträgt oder teilt keine personenbezogenen Daten und nimmt kein Audio auf. Die App arbeitet offline, ohne Telemetrie und Werbung. Sie schaltet nur die Windows-Stummschaltung des Mikrofons um; die optionale Pegelanzeige liest die Lautstärke nur bei geöffnetem Einstellungsfenster und speichert keinen Ton.

Confidentialité (résumé)

Mic Mute Hotkey ne collecte, n’enregistre, ne stocke, ne transmet ni ne partage aucune donnée personnelle ni aucun son. L’application fonctionne hors ligne, sans télémétrie ni publicité. Elle ne fait que couper ou réactiver le micro de Windows ; le vumètre facultatif lit le niveau uniquement lorsque la fenêtre de paramètres est ouverte et ne conserve aucun son.

プライバシー ポリシー (概要)

Mic Mute Hotkey は個人データや音声を収集・録音・保存・送信・共有しません。オフラインで動作し、テレメトリや広告はありません。Windows のマイクのミュートを切り替えるだけです。オプションのレベル メーターは設定画面が開いている間だけ音量を読み取り、音声は保存しません。

Конфиденциальность (кратко)

Mic Mute Hotkey не собирает, не записывает, не хранит, не передаёт и не распространяет персональные данные или звук. Приложение работает офлайн, без телеметрии и рекламы. Оно только включает и выключает микрофон в Windows; необязательный индикатор уровня читает громкость, только пока открыто окно настроек, и не сохраняет звук.
